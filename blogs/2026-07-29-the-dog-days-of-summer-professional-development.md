Sometimes people think the phrase “summer learning” is a bit of an oxymoron, as summertime traditionally is thought of as a break from schooling. Yet summer provides the perfect downtime to engage in a bit of your own learning, whether you want to call that professional development (PD) or personal growth. Beach reading these days can look quite different and can also have a major impact during the new school year!
